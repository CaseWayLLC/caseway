Sales Representative Agreement

This Independent Sales Representative Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of [Effective Date] (the “Effective Date”) by and between CaseWay LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (the “Company”), and [Representative Full Name], an individual (the “Representative”). The Company and the Representative are each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

Caseway is an online directory that connects clients with independent attorneys, owned and operated by the Company. Subscription payments for Caseway listings are collected by Indicium Markets Inc. (the “Payment Collector”) on the Company’s behalf, while the Company pays the commissions due under this Agreement. The Company wishes to engage the Representative, on a non-exclusive basis, to solicit attorneys to purchase a paid Caseway listing, and the Representative wishes to be so engaged, on the terms below.

1. Engagement & Scope

1.1 Appointment. The Company appoints the Representative as a non-exclusive, revocable independent sales representative to introduce and solicit licensed attorneys (“Prospects”) to register for and purchase a paid Caseway subscription listing.

1.2 Non-Exclusive. No territory, quota, or exclusivity is granted. The Company may engage other representatives, sell directly, and pursue any channel without obligation to the Representative.

1.3 No Authority to Bind. The Representative has no authority to bind the Company, set or alter pricing, accept payment, make warranties, or create any obligation on the Company’s behalf. Every sign-up and subscription is subject to the Company’s standard terms, pricing, and approval, including the Company’s right to reject, decline, or remove any listing in its sole discretion.

1.4 Manner of Performance. The Representative controls its own methods, hours, and means of solicitation and supplies its own equipment.

2. Independent Contractor Status

2.1 Status. The Representative is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, joint venturer, or general agent of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

2.2 Taxes & Benefits. The Representative is solely responsible for all income and self-employment taxes. The Representative will deliver a completed IRS Form W-9, and the Company will issue IRS Form 1099 as required by law. The Representative is not entitled to any employee benefits, withholding, workers’ compensation, or unemployment coverage.

2.3 Expenses. The Representative bears its own expenses unless approved in advance in writing by the Company.

3. Definitions

  • “Qualified Attorney” — a Prospect, attributed to the Representative through the Attribution Link, who (a) completes Caseway signup, (b) activates a paid Caseway subscription, and (c) whose first subscription payment is actually collected and cleared (by the Payment Collector on the Company’s behalf). Free signups that never subscribe, self-referrals, and duplicate or fraudulent accounts are excluded.
  • “Collected Revenue” — subscription fees actually received and cleared from a Qualified Attorney by the Payment Collector on the Company’s behalf, net of any refunds, chargebacks, credits, discounts, and payment-processor reversals.
  • “Commission Amount” — the one-time, per-attorney bounty determined under the tier schedule in Section 4, based on the Representative’s cumulative, program-to-date count of Qualified Attorneys.
  • “Attribution Link” — the personal Rewardful referral link and/or coupon code issued to the Representative, through which sign-ups are credited.
  • “Clawback Period” — the thirty (30) days following a Qualified Attorney’s first cleared payment.

4. Commission Structure

4.1 Tiered Bounty. For each Qualified Attorney, the Representative earns a one-time Commission Amount per the schedule below. Rates are marginal: each band’s rate applies only to the Qualified Attorneys counted within that band. Bounties are one-time per Qualified Attorney and are not recurring. Tier position is program-to-date; once a tier is reached, that rate sticks for subsequent attorneys in the band.

Core principle — paid only when we get paid

The Representative’s right to a Commission Amount is funded solely out of, and limited to, the first Collected Revenue received from that Qualified Attorney. The Representative is entitled to the first dollars of Collected Revenue from a Qualified Attorney, up to the applicable Commission Amount. No commission is earned, accrued, or payable except to the extent the corresponding revenue has actually been collected — the Representative is paid only when, and only because, the Company is paid. If the corresponding revenue is never collected, no commission is owed; if Collected Revenue from that attorney is less than the Commission Amount, the commission equals the amounts actually collected as and when received, until the Commission Amount is reached.

Because Caseway listings are billed monthly (for example, the Founding rate of $10/mo), the Collected Revenue from a Qualified Attorney arrives in successive monthly installments. The Commission Amount is therefore funded out of, and collected and paid over, multiple months — not a single month. At the $10/mo Founding rate, a $40 bounty funds over roughly four months of retained payments. In each period the Representative receives the first dollars of that attorney’s Collected Revenue, up to the applicable Commission Amount, until the cap is reached.

4.2 Illustrative Benchmark. The schedule yields approximately $5,000 for every 100 Qualified Attorneys (a $50 blended average), with the effective rate rising toward $65 per attorney at higher volumes. This is a cumulative, lifetime figure that accrues across the funding period as each attorney’s monthly payments are collected — it is not a monthly or up-front amount, and it depends on attorneys being retained long enough for their Commission Amounts to fund in full. Figures are illustrative only and do not guarantee any level of earnings.

5. Tracking & Payment

5.1 Tracking via Rewardful. Sign-ups are credited through the Representative’s Attribution Link; conversions and earnings appear in the Representative’s Rewardful dashboard. The Company’s and Rewardful’s records are the controlling record of Qualified Attorneys, Collected Revenue, and commissions.

5.2 Payment Schedule. Earned and payable commissions are paid monthly via Rewardful, after (a) the corresponding Collected Revenue has been received and (b) the applicable Clawback Period has cleared, subject to any payout minimums and verification. Because a Commission Amount is funded out of the Qualified Attorney’s successive monthly subscription payments, it is paid as that revenue is collected — typically across several monthly cycles, not as a single lump sum in the month the attorney signs up. Each month the Representative receives the portion of the Commission Amount funded by that month’s Collected Revenue, until the cap is reached.

5.3 Statements. The Representative is responsible for reviewing its dashboard and notifying the Company of any discrepancy within thirty (30) days.

6. Clawbacks & Reversals

6.1 30-Day Clawback. If a Qualified Attorney cancels, refunds, charges back, or otherwise fails to remain a paying listing within the Clawback Period, the corresponding Commission Amount (or the collected portion paid) is reversed. Because the Commission Amount funds over successive monthly payments, within the 30-day Clawback Period typically only the first month’s collected slice has actually been paid; the remaining balance continues to be paid month by month as later payments clear, subject to the clawback and offset rights in this Section.

6.2 Offsets. The Company and/or Rewardful may offset reversed amounts against current or future commissions. If commissions are insufficient to cover a reversal, the Representative will repay the shortfall promptly on request.

6.3 No Commission on Reversed Revenue. Commissions are never owed on revenue the Company does not ultimately retain.

7. Conduct & Compliance

7.1 Lawful Solicitation. The Representative will comply with all applicable laws, including anti-spam (e.g., CAN-SPAM), telemarketing (e.g., TCPA), and advertising and consumer-protection laws, and will obtain all required consents.

7.2 Honest Representations. The Representative will make only accurate statements about Caseway, its pricing, and its benefits, and will not guarantee results, case outcomes, or earnings, or make any unauthorized claim.

7.3 No Legal Advice. The Representative is not acting as an attorney, will not provide legal advice, and will not imply any attorney-client relationship.

7.4 No Gaming. Self-referrals, duplicate or fake accounts, incentivized or fraudulent sign-ups, cookie-stuffing, and similar abuse are prohibited and void the related commissions, and may result in immediate termination.

7.5 Brand Use. The Representative will use only Company-approved names, marks, and materials in accordance with the Company’s guidelines and will cease any use on request.

8. Confidentiality

The Representative will keep confidential all non-public Company information (including pricing, prospect lists, methods, and data), use it only to perform under this Agreement, and not disclose it. This obligation survives termination.

9. Intellectual Property

The Company owns the Caseway name, marks, content, and platform. During the Term, the Company grants the Representative a limited, revocable, non-exclusive license to use approved Caseway marks and materials solely for approved solicitation. No other rights are granted, and all goodwill inures to the Company.

10. Term & Termination

10.1 Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

10.2 Termination. Either Party may terminate at any time, with or without cause, on 14 days’ written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for breach, fraud, or legal risk.

10.3 Effect of Termination. The Representative will stop soliciting and using the Company’s marks. For Qualified Attorneys converted before termination, the Representative remains entitled to the unpaid portion of earned Commission Amounts as and when the corresponding Collected Revenue is received and the Clawback Period clears, subject to all clawback and offset rights. No commissions accrue for any sign-up after termination.

10.4 Survival. Sections 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 13 survive termination.

11. Representations & Warranties

Each Party represents that it has full authority to enter into this Agreement. The Representative further represents that it will comply with this Agreement and all applicable laws and is not subject to any restriction that would prevent this engagement.

12. Indemnification & Liability

12.1 Indemnification. The Representative will indemnify and hold the Company harmless from claims, losses, and expenses arising out of the Representative’s breach of this Agreement, misrepresentations, or unlawful conduct.

12.2 Limitation of Liability. To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, and the Company’s aggregate liability is limited to the commissions paid or payable to the Representative in the three (3) months preceding the claim.

13. General

13.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions and materials, including any commission one-pager or marketing summary, which are illustrative only; this Agreement controls.

13.2 Amendments. The Company may update the commission schedule on a prospective basis with reasonable notice; all other changes require a writing signed by both Parties.

13.3 Assignment. The Representative may not assign this Agreement without the Company’s consent; the Company may assign to an affiliate or successor.

13.4 Governing Law; Venue. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Wyoming, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules, and the Parties consent to venue in the State of Wyoming.

13.5 Severability; Waiver. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect; no waiver is effective unless in writing.

13.6 Notices; Counterparts. Notices are effective when sent to the addresses below or on file. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

14. Signatures

The Parties execute this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
