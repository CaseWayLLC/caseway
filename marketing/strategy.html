01 · Executive Summary

A two-sided marketplace built
to compound.

Caseway is a Zillow-style marketplace that connects people who need legal help with solo and independent attorneys. Clients answer two plain-language questions and browse matching lawyers on an interactive map. Attorneys list their practice for free and pay only to stay visible.

Our strategy rests on a single idea: grow supply with zero friction, capture demand with local SEO, and convert visibility into recurring revenue. Intake is free, so attorneys join with nothing to lose. Each new attorney expands our geographic coverage, which spins up new local landing pages, which pull in high-intent clients searching for legal help nearby. When attorneys see real clients arrive, they subscribe to stay listed and refer their peers — and the loop tightens.

Three compounding engines drive the plan: a Founding Member offer that fills supply fast and rewards early believers, an attorney-to-attorney referral program that turns tight professional networks into a growth channel, and programmatic local SEO that manufactures thousands of high-intent landing pages automatically as we scale.

02 · Opportunity

A large, fragmented market
that incumbents overcharge.

Most U.S. attorneys are solo or small-firm practitioners with little marketing budget and no time for SEO or ad management. Meanwhile, people searching for a lawyer face directories that are cluttered, pay-to-win, or hard to trust. That gap is the opportunity.

The problem, both sides feel it

C For clients

Finding a lawyer is opaque and stressful, usually in a difficult moment. Legacy directories bury small practices under big-firm ad spend, jargon is everywhere, and there is no simple way to compare nearby options or judge who to trust.

A For attorneys

Getting found online is expensive and technical. SEO, ad campaigns, and directory fees eat thin solo margins, and incumbents bundle solos alongside large firms they cannot outbid. Most solos simply rely on word of mouth.

Why now

  • Underserved supply. Solo and independent attorneys are the majority of the profession and the least served by existing legal-marketing products.
  • High-intent demand. People searching "{practice area} attorney near me" are ready to act — the highest-converting traffic in legal.
  • Incumbent fatigue. FindLaw, Avvo, and Justia are seen as costly and cluttered, leaving room for a clean, solo-first, transparently priced alternative.
  • A defensible wedge. A map-first, plain-language experience purpose-built for solos is hard for generalist directories to copy without alienating their big-firm advertisers.

The three compounding engines

1 Founding Member offer

A locked-in $10/mo rate for the first 10,000 attorneys creates urgency and scarcity, fills supply fast, and converts early adopters into loyal evangelists.

2 Referral program

Attorneys name who referred them; referrers rank higher and climb a leaderboard. Tight professional networks — bar groups, alumni, local bars — become a viral channel.

3 Programmatic local SEO

"{Practice Area} Attorneys in {County}, {State}" pages are generated automatically and grow with every new attorney and location — a content moat that expands itself, capturing long-tail, high-intent search at near-zero marginal cost.

06 · Supply Acquisition

Winning the attorneys.

Supply comes first — a directory with no lawyers helps no one. We remove every reason to say no, then give attorneys reasons to bring their peers.

Playbook

  • Free, no-risk intake. Listing costs nothing and carries no upfront charge; attorneys pay only when they choose to go live and stay visible.
  • Founding Member urgency. The first 10,000 attorneys lock in $10/mo — a once-only, scarcity-driven reason to join now rather than later.
  • Peer referrals. A built-in referral program rewards attorneys who bring colleagues with higher ranking and leaderboard status.
  • A clear upgrade path. Three tiers let attorneys start cheap and step up to premium placement as they see returns.
  • Direct & partnership outreach. Bar-association partnerships, CLE events, and targeted LinkedIn/email to solos seed the earliest cohorts.

! Why "pay to be seen" works

Attorneys experience value before they pay. A free listing plus inbound local traffic proves ROI, so the subscription feels like keeping a working channel on — not gambling on an ad.

Activation metric that matters

The key conversion is list → subscribe. Everything on the supply side — onboarding, the Founding offer, lead notifications — is engineered to move attorneys across that line and keep them renewing.

07 · Demand Acquisition

Winning the clients.

Client acquisition is led by organic search. We turn supply into thousands of pages that match exactly what people type when they need a lawyer — then convert that traffic with trust.

Channels & tactics

S Programmatic SEO

Auto-generated location and practice-area landing pages (100+ live and scaling) capture long-tail "near me" searches — the cheapest, highest-intent traffic in legal.

L Local & technical SEO

Structured data (JSON-LD), clean sitemaps, and a fast experience help every county, profile, and practice-area page get indexed and rank.

G Content & guides

Plain-language explainers ("how to find a divorce lawyer," "what to ask in a free consultation") build authority and capture top-of-funnel demand.

T Trust & conversion

Verification badges, a free-consultation CTA, and client reviews turn visitors into booked consultations — the metric attorneys actually pay for.
