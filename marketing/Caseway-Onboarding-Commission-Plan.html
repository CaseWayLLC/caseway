What counts as a qualified attorney

  • The attorney completes signup and activates a paid Caseway subscription — the bounty begins funding when their first payment clears, then accrues as later monthly payments collect.
  • Free signups that never subscribe do not count.
  • 30-day clawback: if they cancel or refund within 30 days, the bounty is reversed.
  • No self-referrals or duplicate accounts.

How you get paid — Rewardful

  • You receive a personal referral link & coupon; share it with attorneys you pitch.
  • Conversions and earnings appear in your Rewardful dashboard in real time.
  • Payouts run monthly via Rewardful as each attorney’s revenue is collected — typically across several cycles, not a lump sum — after the 30-day clawback window clears.
  • Tiers are program-to-date — once reached, the rate sticks.
